Security Architecture

One school. One data store.
Total isolation.

Unlike platforms that store all schools in a single shared database, Eduxo provisions a dedicated, isolated data store for every school. Your students' data never resides alongside another school's data — not even on the same logical layer.

  • ✅ Dedicated data store per school
  • ✅ School-scoped authentication
  • ✅ Role & permission-based access control
  • ✅ Encrypted at rest and in transit

Setup Fee

One-Time

Every school pays a one-time ₹5,000 setup fee at registration, covering dedicated database provisioning, environment configuration, and onboarding support.

  • 🗄️ Dedicated database & environment setup
  • 🧑‍💻 Guided onboarding support for your team
  • 💳 Paid securely once via Razorpay
  • 🔁 Never billed again for setup
  • 🚫 No ads shown to fund the platform

Subscription

Monthly or Yearly

After setup, schools keep access active with a simple subscription — Monthly at ₹599/month or Yearly at ₹6,988/year (save 3%). Every plan unlocks the complete platform, with no feature paywalls.

  • ✅ Every feature included, on every plan
  • ✅ Pay Once each cycle, or enable UPI Autopay
  • ✅ Switch between Monthly and Yearly anytime
  • ✅ Priority support on Yearly billing
  • ✅ GST-compliant invoicing
